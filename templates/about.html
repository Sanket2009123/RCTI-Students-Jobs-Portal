WHO WE ARE

Empowering Students to Build Their Future

RCTI Students Jobs Portal is a modern career platform created to make the journey from education to employment simpler, smarter and more accessible.

Students can explore job opportunities, discover internships, apply for suitable positions, upload resumes and take important steps towards building their professional careers.

At the same time, companies can connect with talented students and discover candidates who are ready to learn, contribute and grow.
