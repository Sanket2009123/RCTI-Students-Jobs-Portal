Who We Are

RCTI Students Jobs Portal is a modern recruitment platform designed especially for RCTI students. Students can search internships, apply for jobs, upload resumes and build their careers while companies can easily hire talented students.

🎯 Our Mission

To provide students with quality internships, placement opportunities and industry connections.

🚀 Our Vision

To become the most trusted student recruitment portal connecting colleges and industries.

💡 Our Goal

Help students build successful careers while helping companies recruit skilled candidates.
